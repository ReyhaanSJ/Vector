VECTOR - your laptop, on command
================================

WINDOWS 10 / 11
  Double-click  "Install VECTOR.bat"
  (If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info > Run anyway.)

MAC (macOS 11 or newer)
  Double-click  "Install VECTOR.command"
  (If macOS says it can't be opened, right-click it > Open > Open.)

Then answer the questions. Pressing Enter picks the suggested answer.
Setup copies VECTOR to its own folder, so you can delete this folder afterwards.

After installing
  Press Ctrl+Shift+Space anywhere and type what you want, or just say "Vector, ...".
  Type /guide in the bar for the illustrated guide.

Help, updates and the full manual:  https://github.com/ReyhaanSJ/Vector
